While designing the levels I have encountered several problems. The problems I have encountered was aligning the obstacles and the grounds, changing the axis of obstacles and adjusting diffuclty level. I design these levels by putting each object one by one and aligning them with each other. I think this is not the most efficient way to do it. It took long time and it is easy to make mistakes. I plan to get better at level design. To achive that I need to design more and look for better ways online.
