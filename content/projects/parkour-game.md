### Watch the gameplay!

If the video doesn't load, refresh the page!

<iframe src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/2Y7Ja8avpZ0" title="YouTube video" allowfullscreen=""></iframe>

This game is inspired by hypercasul games, American Ninja Warrior and WipeOut. But there is a little twist to it. The zombies. The player's goal is to kill the zombies, collect the stars that the zombies drop and make it to finish line. The first 5 level of the game is designed and they are playable. Time to move on to the next project!

Check out the game files on GitHub!
