##### Designing for Different Dimensions & Others

**Redesigning a Local Car Repair Website**

This project focuses on creating a mobile-friendly design tailored for SBU students and staff.

**Key Features:**

**• User-Centered Design:** Simplified navigation for booking services and accessing contact information.

**• SBU Branding:** Incorporated school colors and logo for familiarity and trust.

The redesign improves accessibility and usability for the local community.
