##### Design for Collaboration

**Attendance Verification System**

Designed to ensure fair and privacy-conscious attendance tracking for campus events.

**Key Features:**

• **Geolocation Tracking:** Verifies attendance without exposing exact locations.

• **Email Verification:** Adds accountability while preventing multiple submissions.

• **Privacy Focus:** Anonymized records and clear data usage policies build trust.

This system balances usability, fairness, and privacy for event participants.
