# About me

With over 14 years of competitive swimming experience, I’ve had the honor of competing at the Division I level in the United States. This journey has taught me invaluable lessons in dedication, teamwork, and leadership—skills I bring into every project I undertake.

As both a gamer and a developer, I have always been fascinated by how software and games are created, and my passion for understanding the development process has grown over the years. This interest has led me to pursue a future in computer science, where I aim to contribute to both game development and software engineering fields.

Whether building gameplay systems or developing software solutions, I pride myself on my ability to bring people together, work collaboratively, and see projects through from concept to completion. I approach each responsibility with a commitment to excellence, ensuring tasks are done efficiently, effectively, and with a sense of pride in the outcome.

My strong foundation in problem-solving, paired with my technical expertise, drives me to continually seek out new challenges—whether in the realm of game development or software engineering. I’m excited to contribute my skills to innovative projects that push boundaries and create meaningful impact.
