##### Identifying Needs through Interviews

This project addresses user frustrations with repetitive content and privacy concerns on social media platforms.

**Key Features:**

**• Algorithm Transparency:** Users can adjust feed content percentages and control algorithm settings.

**• Simplified Privacy Agreements:** Short, clear agreements ensure understanding and build trust.

By prioritizing user control and data privacy, the app creates a balanced and transparent social media experience.
